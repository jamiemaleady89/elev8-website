02 Classes

Train to a Beat

Four class formats on a dedicated studio floor — from full-tilt cardio to official hybrid race training — plus retirees and ladies-only slots. Book by session pack or bundle with your membership.

Cardio

Spin

Ride to the beat in a dark, red-lit studio. Climbs, sprints and intervals on commercial bikes — every level in the same room, at the same tempo.

  • 45 min
  • All levels
Cardio

Step Aerobics

Old-school conditioning that still works. Choreographed step routines that build coordination, stamina and serious leg endurance.

  • 45 min
  • All levels
Dance Fitness

Zumba®

Latin rhythms, zero judgement. A dance-fitness party that burns like a workout and feels like a night out.

  • 45 min
  • No experience needed
Hybrid Race

HYROX / TRYKA

Hybrid race training on official HYROX centre equipment — sled push and pull, ski erg, rower, wall balls, lunges and burpee broad jumps. The exact stations you'll meet on race day.

  • 35–45 min
  • Scalable

Also on the timetable

Dedicated

Retirees Classes

Daytime sessions built for older members — mobility, balance, and steady strength work at a sensible pace, in a room of people the same age.

Dedicated

Ladies-Only Classes

Women-only slots on the timetable for anyone who trains more comfortably that way — same coaching, same equipment, own space.

03 Facilities

Recover. Refuel.

The work doesn't stop at the last rep. On-site contrast therapy to bring you back down, and a proper cafe to get you back up.

Outdoor Recovery

Outdoor Sauna & Plunge Pools

Full contrast therapy out on the terrace. Sweat it out in the barrel sauna, drop your core temperature in the plunge pools, go again — the half of training most gyms leave you to sort out yourself.

  • 8Person barrel sauna
  • 2Chilled plunge pools

Both plunge pools are temperature-controlled, so the cold is the same every session — no guessing, no waiting for a refill.

The Cafe

Flexpresso

A proper cafe first — flat whites, americanos, cappuccinos, made properly — with a protein twist for anyone who wants one.

  • Coffee, done right — the full espresso menu, no compromise.
  • Custom protein shakes — built around your favourite flavours.
  • Optional add-ins — throw in a scoop of creatine or your pick of extras.
  • Protein bars & snacks — the popular brands, in stock.
  • Supplements & protein — whey, lean protein and daily essentials, sold over the counter.

Come in for a coffee whether you're training that day or not.
