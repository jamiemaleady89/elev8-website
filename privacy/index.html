Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

01 Who we are

02 What data we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address and phone number (e.g. through a Facebook or Instagram form, email, direct message or phone call)
  • Class preferences and booking details
  • Membership and payment details when you join (handled by our membership provider, Ashbourne Membership Management)
  • Anonymous website usage statistics (e.g. pages visited). These do not identify you and do not use cookies.

03 Why we use it

  • To book you into classes and contact you about your booking (legal basis: your consent, given when you submit a form)
  • To follow up with you about membership (legal basis: your consent)
  • To manage your membership and payments if you join (legal basis: performance of a contract)
  • To avoid showing our ads to existing members, we may share a securely encrypted (hashed) list of member contact details with Meta (legal basis: our legitimate interest in running relevant advertising)
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it (legal basis: legitimate interest)

04 Who we share it with

We only share your data with service providers who help us run the gym:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) — advertising and lead forms
  • Ashbourne Membership Management — membership sign-ups and payments
  • Vercel — website hosting and anonymous analytics

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, they use approved safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

We never sell your personal data.

05 How long we keep it

  • If you contact us or book a class but don't become a member, we delete your details within 6 months.
  • If you become a member, we keep your details for as long as you are a member, and afterwards only as long as required by law (e.g. financial records).

06 Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. To do any of these, email elev8fitnessgyms@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission (www.dataprotection.ie).

07 Cookies

This website does not currently use cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

08 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
